Privacy policy

Aerium does not collect, store or sell your personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the Aerium browser for Android, wherever you got it (Google Play, GitHub or Obtainium), and the website aeriumbrowser.com. Aerium is an independent project, not a company.

What we collect

Nothing. Aerium has no servers of its own that receive data from the app. We do not see what you browse, search, type, download or install, and we cannot tell who uses Aerium.

  • No usage statistics. Chromium's usage and crash reporting is switched off.
  • No account or sync. Signing in to Google or any other account inside the browser is disabled.
  • No advertising ID. Aerium does not read or use it.
  • Crash details stay on your phone. The last crash is kept so you can see it in About Aerium. It is only shared if you copy it and send it to us yourself.

What stays on your phone

Your history, bookmarks, open tabs, saved passwords, cookies, site settings, extensions and Aerium settings are stored only on your device. Aerogel tabs keep nothing after you close them. Backups are files that you save and keep yourself. You can delete all of this at any time from Settings, or by removing the app.

Connections Aerium makes

A browser has to talk to the internet. These are the connections Aerium makes, and why:

  • Websites you visit. Like any browser, Aerium connects to the sites you open. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Aerium sends Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track with every request, blocks third party cookies by default and upgrades connections to HTTPS.
  • Your search engine. What you search for goes to the search engine you choose. DuckDuckGo is the default. If search suggestions are on, what you type in the address bar is sent to that engine as you type.
  • Secure DNS. If you pick a secure DNS provider during setup or in Settings, the names of the sites you visit are looked up through that provider instead of your network's.
  • Extension stores. When you install or update an extension, it is downloaded from the store it comes from, such as the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons, Opera Add-ons, or GitHub for uBlock Origin Lite. Each extension follows its own privacy policy, so only install extensions you trust.
  • Update check. Copies of Aerium from GitHub ask GitHub once a day whether a newer version exists. Nothing is downloaded or installed without you. You can turn this off in About Aerium. Copies installed from Google Play do not make this check and are updated by Google Play.
  • Engine components. Some parts of the Chromium engine, such as security certificate lists, can be updated from Google's servers. These requests do not include your browsing.

Google Safe Browsing, link preloading and Google's background services are switched off by default.

Permissions

Aerium only asks for a permission when a site or feature needs it, for example the camera for a video call, your location for a map, or notifications. Android shows you each request and you can change your answer later in Android settings or in Aerium's site settings.

Google Play

If you install Aerium from Google Play, Google handles the download, updates and any reviews you write, under Google's own privacy policy. Aerium does not receive information about you from Google.

This website

aeriumbrowser.com has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address to run and protect the service. The download section asks GitHub's public API for the latest release, so your browser connects to GitHub when you open it.

Children

Aerium is a general web browser and is not made for children. Since we collect nothing, we hold no data about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. The full history is public in this website's repository on GitHub.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. To report a privacy or security problem privately, use the private report form.
