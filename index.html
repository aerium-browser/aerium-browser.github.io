A browser that stays out of the way.

No telemetry calling home, no bundled Google services, no ad platform baked into the settings page. Built on Chromium, stripped of what doesn't belong to you.

Get Aerium View source

Named after the lightest solid there is.

Aerium takes its name from aerogel, a material that is up to 99% air by volume, solid enough to hold its shape, light enough to rest on a flower without bending the stem. That is the standard the browser is held to: strip away the weight that does not need to be there, keep what actually works.

Built by Dioide on top of Chromium, using ungoogled-chromium on Windows and Linux, and a Vanadium-based fork on Android, with a curated set of privacy defaults and a browser identity of its own, not a reskin.
